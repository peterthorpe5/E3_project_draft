2. GOAL
-------

Determine which sequence, domain, residue, fold and pocket characteristics
distinguish experimentally supported E3 ubiquitin-ligase systems from suitable
controls, while respecting the fact that “E3” is not one homogeneous protein
family and does not have one universal short motif.

The workflow must answer these separate questions:

  A. Which signatures recur within a defined E3 mechanistic class or role?
  B. Which signatures are enriched relative to matched non-E3 controls?
  C. Which signatures generalise to held-out HOGs, species and clades?
  D. Which residues or structural regions are conserved within a family?
  E. Which candidate members best represent the observed family signature?
  F. Where is evidence unavailable, ambiguous or sample-limited?

The output is evidence for prioritisation and hypothesis generation. It is not
proof that a candidate is an E3, binds a ligand or ubiquitinates a substrate.

3. REQUIRED E3 STRATIFICATION
-----------------------------

Never pool all labelled E3-related proteins into a single motif discovery set.
Create an explicit controlled vocabulary with at least these mechanistic classes
and component roles:

  RING E3 ligases
    - Single-subunit RING proteins, including plant RING families and TRIM-like
      proteins where supported.
    - RING domains primarily recruit an E2 and promote direct ubiquitin transfer;
      the E3 generally does not form a catalytic ubiquitin thioester.

  U-box E3 ligases
    - RING-like proteins such as PUB/CHIP-related classes.
    - Analyse separately from canonical RING before considering a combined
      RING-like stratum.

  HECT E3 ligases
    - Catalytic HECT-domain proteins with an active-site cysteine and an E3–Ub
      thioester intermediate.
    - Analyse HECT N-lobe/E2-binding and C-lobe/catalytic regions explicitly.

  RBR E3 ligases
    - RING1–IBR/between-RING–RING2 architecture.
    - Hybrid mechanism: RING1 recruits E2 and RING2 contributes the catalytic
      cysteine. Treating RBR as ordinary RING would obscure this distinction.

  Cullin–RING ligase systems
    - Cullin scaffold.
    - RBX/RING catalytic component.
    - Adaptor components.
    - Substrate receptors, including F-box, BTB, DCAF/DDB1-associated,
      VHL-box and SOCS-box classes where applicable.
    - Do not label every receptor or adaptor as an independently catalytic E3.
      Preserve component_role separately from system_class.

  Other or atypical ubiquitin E3 mechanisms
    - Retain only with explicit source evidence and a named mechanism/class.
    - Do not force uncertain proteins into RING, HECT or RBR.

  Ubiquitin-like modifier ligases
    - SUMO, NEDD8, UFM1 and other UBL systems must be separate strata, not
      silently mixed with ubiquitin E3s.

  Putative, pseudoenzyme or E3-associated records
    - Keep separate evidence states for putative catalytic E3, non-catalytic
      complex component, E3-associated protein, pseudoenzyme and unknown.

  PHD and other zinc-binding domains
    - A PHD/RING-like fold or annotation alone is not universal proof of E3
      activity. Require curated role evidence and retain ambiguity explicitly.

Minimum controlled fields:
  e3_system_class
  e3_mechanistic_class
  component_role
  e3_family
  evidence_status
  evidence_source
  evidence_reference
  active_site_expected
  active_site_residue
  curation_status
  curation_reason

Recommended curation_status values:
  REVIEWED_POSITIVE
  REVIEWED_NEGATIVE
  REVIEWED_COMPONENT_NOT_CATALYTIC
  PROPOSED
  AMBIGUOUS
  UNMAPPED
  EXCLUDED

4. SCIENTIFIC INPUTS
--------------------

Every run must consume immutable, checksummed manifests. Never discover inputs
by taking whichever file happens to be newest.

Required inputs:

  1. Candidate/HOG manifest
     - Exact HOG IDs and authoritative pre-structure ranks.
     - Campaign identifier and parent E3 release identifier.
     - Explicit inclusion interval or exact selected-HOG list.
     - Do not infer whether a cluster campaign has run from the app version.

  2. Protein sequences
     - Exact source identifier, normalised accession and source species label.
     - Full-length sequence and checksum.
     - Sequence completeness and fragment status.
     - HOG, OG and discovery-cluster links kept as distinct fields.

  3. Curated E3 authority
     - Known positives, mechanistic class, family and component role.
     - Evidence/provenance and review status.
     - Records from the 2026 UPS survey must enter as proposed until reviewed;
       they must not silently replace the established seed authority.

  4. Domain evidence
     - InterPro/Pfam accessions, descriptions, coordinates and assessment state.
     - Exact E3-domain catalogue version and checksum.

  5. Structural evidence, where available
     - Exact model accession/version and coordinate checksum.
     - ModelCIF residue-level pLDDT and PAE provenance.
     - Selected pockets and residue mappings.
     - Existing pairwise TM-align/US-align outputs and transformations.
     - Full-model and retained-core values must remain separate.

  6. Orthology/taxonomy bridge from orthofinder-results
     - Exact source label, accepted name, taxon ID and full ordered lineage.
     - HOG membership and optional gene/species-tree distances.
     - Mapping and review states.
     - Never infer taxonomy from underscore replacement alone.

  7. Negative and comparison sets
     - Matched non-E3 proteins with similar length, taxonomic composition,
       domain complexity, subcellular context and structure availability.
     - Within-superfamily non-E3 or non-catalytic controls where defensible.
     - Shuffled sequences may test composition artefacts but are not sufficient
       as the only biological negative set.

6. REQUIRED WORKFLOW STAGES
---------------------------

Stage 00 — validate and freeze inputs
  - Validate schemas, identifiers, sequences, coordinates and checksums.
  - Reject duplicate identities with conflicting sequences or annotations.
  - Emit an immutable campaign manifest and normalised configuration.
  - Record all unavailable evidence explicitly.

Stage 01 — assign reviewed E3 strata
  - Join curated class, family and component-role authority.
  - Produce overlap/disagreement tables for old versus new curation sources.
  - Keep proposed records out of positive training sets by default.

Stage 02 — build analysis sequence sets
  - Publish full-length, domain-only and optional retained-core FASTA separately.
  - Retained-core FASTA must record exact original coordinates and rule.
  - Never trim through a mapped catalytic/E3 domain or protected pocket residue
    without an explicit exclusion or reviewed override.
  - Deduplicate exact sequences and cluster near-redundant sequences.

Stage 03 — partition without leakage
  - Split discovery/training and validation by HOG or higher taxonomic clade,
    not by random individual sequence alone.
  - A near-identical paralogue must not appear in both discovery and validation.
  - Freeze split manifests before motif discovery.

Stage 04 — discover sequence motifs
  - Run MEME for richer motif models where sample size permits.
  - Run STREME for differential enriched short motifs.
  - Scan independent sets with FIMO.
  - Use AME/SEA or an equivalent documented enrichment test where appropriate.
  - Record program/version, container digest, occurrence model, motif widths,
    background model, strand policy, random seed, command argv and checksums.
  - Run family/domain strata separately before any pooled exploratory analysis.

Stage 05 — build profile models
  - Build family/domain multiple alignments with a pinned aligner.
  - Construct and calibrate HMMER profiles when a dispersed domain signature is
    more biologically plausible than a short motif.
  - Compare profile-HMM and short-motif discrimination on held-out data.

Stage 06 — quantify evolutionary residue conservation
  - Estimate site-specific evolutionary rates from a versioned multiple
    alignment and tree (Rate4Site/ConSurf-like method or a local equivalent).
  - Map scores back to exact sequence and structure residue coordinates.
  - This is different from EMERALD pairwise alignment safety windows.
  - Record mapping failures, gaps and model-coordinate discontinuities.

Stage 07 — evaluate structural signatures
  - Compare fold/domain architecture within each mechanistic class.
  - Use pinned Foldseek and/or US-align/TM-align workflows as appropriate.
  - Compare conserved pockets, catalytic residues and class-specific geometry.
  - Retain pLDDT/PAE and coverage so low-confidence geometry is not over-read.
  - Do not treat a common globular fold as sufficient proof of E3 function.

Stage 08 — optional independent disorder evidence
  - Integrate a locally runnable, licence-compatible predictor only after its
    exact version, model files and redistribution terms are recorded.
  - IUPred2A or another reviewed predictor may be evaluated.
  - Keep predictor output separate from AlphaFold pLDDT; neither is ground truth.
  - A web button marked “coming soon” is not an implemented evidence source.

Stage 09 — validate signatures
  - Evaluate held-out positives and matched negatives.
  - Report sensitivity, specificity, precision, recall, MCC, PR-AUC and ROC-AUC
    only when sample sizes and class balance make them interpretable.
  - Bootstrap at the HOG level where possible.
  - Report confidence intervals and per-class sample counts.
  - Include ablations: motif alone, domain alone, structural signature alone and
    combinations.

Stage 10 — publish an immutable result bundle
  - Write normalised TSV and Parquet tables, FASTA, MEME XML/text, HMM profiles,
    Newick trees, SVG/PDF figures and portable structure assets.
  - Do not use CSV for scientific data exchange.
  - Add SHA-256 checksums and a complete provenance manifest.
  - Publish app-facing tables only after schema and reconciliation tests pass.

10. INCREMENTAL AND RESTARTABLE EXECUTION
-----------------------------------------

The cache key for one unit of work must include:
  input content checksum
  normalised configuration checksum
  code commit
  tool version
  container digest where used
  relevant reference-database checksum

Write to a staging path, validate, then promote atomically. Never consider the
existence of a directory sufficient evidence that a task completed. A completed
record requires a success manifest plus validated expected outputs.

Retries must be per HOG/member/stratum where practical. One failed record must
not erase successful independent work.

11. CLUSTER TEMPORARY-DIRECTORY RULE
------------------------------------

On the cluster, perform heavy temporary work inside the scheduler-provided
TMPDIR, then validate and copy results to a persistent working directory.
Never hard-code /tmp. The Mac workflow must not require /tmp either.

Example cluster pattern (replace persistent paths and scheduler directives):

  set -Eeuo pipefail
  : "${TMPDIR:?The cluster must provide TMPDIR}"
  PERSISTENT_RUN="/persistent/path/e3_family_signatures/campaign_001"
  JOB_TOKEN="${SLURM_JOB_ID:-manual}"
  JOB_WORK_DIR="${TMPDIR}/e3_family_signatures_${JOB_TOKEN}"

  mkdir -p "${JOB_WORK_DIR}" "${PERSISTENT_RUN}"
  rsync -a "/persistent/path/frozen_inputs/" "${JOB_WORK_DIR}/inputs/"

  e3-family-signatures run-all \
    --config "${JOB_WORK_DIR}/inputs/campaign.yaml" \
    --output-dir "${JOB_WORK_DIR}/results" \
    --resume

  e3-family-signatures validate-inputs \
    --config "${JOB_WORK_DIR}/inputs/campaign.yaml" \
    --output-dir "${JOB_WORK_DIR}/results"

  rsync -a "${JOB_WORK_DIR}/results/" "${PERSISTENT_RUN}/"

Do not use `--delete` in handover rsync commands. Log the source and destination,
copy checksums, and verify the persistent copy before the job exits.

12. CODING AND QUALITY STANDARDS
--------------------------------

Mandatory requirements from the project owner:

  - Python 3.11 or newer.
  - PEP 8 compliant; use the repository's declared maximum line length.
  - Google-style docstrings for every module, class and function.
  - Type hints for public and internal function signatures.
  - Named Python arguments in workflow calls; avoid opaque positional APIs.
  - Unit tests for every new function, including private validation helpers.
  - Synthetic end-to-end tests covering a complete small campaign.
  - Defensive input/schema/range/identifier validation.
  - Structured, informative logging at stage, stratum and work-unit level.
  - No secrets, credentials or machine-specific paths in committed code.
  - No silent exception swallowing and no broad `except Exception` unless it
    re-raises with context at a top-level boundary.
  - No biological missing value converted to zero/False without a documented
    scientific rule.
  - Read-only inputs; staged and atomic outputs.
  - TSV rather than CSV for tabular scientific interchange.
  - UK English in user-facing text and documentation.
  - Deterministic ordering, random seeds and output names.
  - Pin external command versions/containers and record their argv safely.

Recommended local quality gate:

  python -m compileall -q src tests
  python -m pycodestyle src tests --max-line-length=100
  python -m pydocstyle --convention=google src/e3_family_signatures
  python -m coverage erase
  python -m coverage run --branch -m pytest -q
  python -m coverage report --fail-under=95

  - checksums and stable manifest serialisation;
  - identifier normalisation and collision rejection;

  - full/domain/core coordinate mapping;

  - atomic publication and checksum inventory;

Integration tests must run pinned tiny fixtures through each external adapter.
If a third-party binary is unavailable in normal CI, keep parser/argv tests
mandatory and place a clearly labelled container smoke test in optional CI.

The synthetic end-to-end test must include:
  - at least two E3 mechanistic classes;
  - a catalytic component and a non-catalytic complex component;
  - matched negatives;
  - two HOGs and at least two taxa;
  - an unreviewed mapping;
  - missing structure and missing-domain cases;
  - a successful motif/signature result and a no-significant-signature result;
  - a rerun proving completed work is reused;
  - exact reconciliation to the publication manifest.

Maintain docs/TEST_TRACEABILITY.tsv mapping every function and acceptance rule
to tests.

14. APP-FACING INTEGRATION
--------------------------

Do not make Streamlit execute MEME, HMMER, Foldseek or large alignments. The
workflow publishes compact, immutable relations. A later E3 Python app release
may add a “Family signatures” results page that reads:

  motif_catalogue
  motif_occurrences
  profile_hmm_hits
  residue_conservation
  structural_signature_summary
  member_signature_readiness
  signature_validation_summary

The app must display discovery and held-out validation separately. It must show
class/role, sample size, availability state, provenance and warnings beside any
logo, score or structure projection.

EMERALD remains a useful external pairwise review hand-off. It is not a
replacement for multi-sequence evolutionary conservation, family-stratified
motif discovery or independent biological-disorder prediction.

16. MINIMUM ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA FOR v0.1.0
------------------------------------------

  1. A clean checkout installs with documented commands on macOS and Linux.
  2. The full quality gate passes at or above 95% statement/branch coverage as
     reported by the repository command.
  3. A tiny offline end-to-end campaign runs twice; the second run reuses every
     checksum-compatible completed unit.
  4. RING/U-box, HECT, RBR and Cullin-system component roles remain separate.
  5. Training/validation leakage by exact sequence, redundancy cluster and HOG
     is detected and rejected.
  6. Every external result is tied to tool/reference versions and checksums.
  7. Missing, failed, excluded and assessed-negative states remain distinct.
  8. Motif and profile results are validated against held-out HOGs or clades.
  9. Publication reconciles exactly to frozen inputs and has SHA-256 inventory.
 10. App-facing tables are documented, bounded and contain no machine-local
     temporary paths.

17. OUT OF SCOPE FOR THE FIRST RELEASE
--------------------------------------

  - Running heavy analysis interactively in Streamlit.
  - Replacing the authoritative E3 HOG rank.
  - Declaring one motif universal to every E3 mechanism.
  - Treating low AlphaFold pLDDT as proven disorder.
  - Calling every Cullin-system component a catalytic E3.
  - Scraping browser-only EMERALD results into the authority.
  - Reimplementing generic OrthoFinder parsing or the selection-coverage tree.
  - Assuming that absence from a sampled dataset means biological absence.
