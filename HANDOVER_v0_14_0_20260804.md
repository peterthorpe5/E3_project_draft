  are claimed;
- `METHOD_STATUS.tsv` and `MILESTONE_COVERAGE.tsv` record that boundary.

- MIT package and DuckDB, MPL-2.0 Gemmi and optional BSD-3-Clause RDKit;
- fail-closed SPDX allow-list and `allow_restricted_licence_tools: false`;
- `COMPONENT_LICENCES.tsv` is published on every run.
