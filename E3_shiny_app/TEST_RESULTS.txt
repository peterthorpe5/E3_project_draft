==================================================

Validation completed in the build environment on 2026-08-05:

- All 53 application, helper and test R files passed the native base-R parser
  through the R 4.6.0 WebAssembly runtime.
- The independent tree-sitter R grammar also reported no syntax errors.
- Six core v0.9.0 selector and SQL-builder checks executed inside base R,
  including evolutionary-group rank preference, bounded candidate queries,
  expression-unit isolation, complete tissue aggregation and volcano inputs.

  executed with DuckDB against the corrected-expression authority containing
  32,981,864 context rows. For `N0.HOG0000376`, it returned 231 heatmap cells,
  the requested ten-row exact preview and 249 complete species/tissue cells.
  The complete profile counted more source rows than the preview limit, proving
  that the graph is aggregated before the exact-row cap.
- The repository-level suite passed all 16 tests.
- `git diff --check` must be rerun after applying the distribution overlay.

Environment limitation:

- The build environment does not contain the native R package stack required by
  Shiny/testthat (bslib, dplyr, DT, duckplyr, ggplot2 and plotly). The complete
  application suite must therefore be executed in the Mac `e3_shiny_app`
  environment before committing:

    Rscript E3_shiny_app/inst/scripts/check_dependencies.R
    Rscript E3_shiny_app/inst/scripts/run_tests.R
